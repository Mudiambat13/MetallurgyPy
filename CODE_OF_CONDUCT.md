# Code of conduct

We want MetallurgyPy to be a respectful, technically honest community.

- Be courteous. Criticise code, equations and results, not people.
- Be honest about what has and has not been verified. Do not present a simplified model as
  a validated one, and do not invent constants, data or references.
- Welcome questions from students and practising engineers alike; English and French are both fine.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that break these rules.
Report a problem privately to the maintainer (GitHub: Mudiambat13).
